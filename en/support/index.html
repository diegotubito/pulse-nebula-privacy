Help

Pulse Nebula support

Get help with your account, workspace, access, privacy or the mobile app.

Email us
pulsenebula.tech@gmail.com

What to include

  • Your account email or username.
  • Your workspace name, if the issue is workspace-specific.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Your device and app version when reporting a mobile issue.

Never send your password, verification codes, payment-card details, biometric information or other secrets. We may ask you to verify account ownership before making security-sensitive changes.

Account access

If you cannot sign in, use the password-recovery option first. If your workspace access was removed or suspended, contact your organization’s workspace owner or administrator; Pulse Nebula support does not silently override an organization’s access decisions.

Privacy and account deletion

For privacy questions, write to the same support address. To remove your account, follow the account deletion instructions.

Languages

Support is available in English and Spanish.
