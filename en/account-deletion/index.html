Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Pulse Nebula account from within the app or through the email pathway below. You do not need to reinstall the app to submit a request.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Pulse Nebula and sign in
  2. Open Settings
  3. Select Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion request

Request deletion without the app

Send an email from the address registered to your Pulse Nebula account to pulsenebula.tech@gmail.com with the subject “Pulse Nebula account deletion request” and include your username. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the request. Do not send your password, payment-card information or other secrets.

Data removal

If you no longer have access to your registered email or cannot sign in, you may still contact us so that another way to verify your identity can be assessed. Do not send an identity-document photo or other sensitive information unless its necessity and an appropriate method have been explained.

When deletion succeeds, Pulse Nebula removes the user account, its personal account record and refresh tokens. Business, financial and audit records owned by a workspace are not deleted by this action and may retain stable actor references when required for operational integrity, legal compliance, security or auditability. Deleting a user account does not delete the workspace or data belonging to other workspace users.

If the account is the last active System Owner, Pulse Nebula will reject deletion because at least one active System Owner must remain. Another System Owner must be assigned before the request can be completed.

Contact

Deleting your personal account does not automatically cancel purchases, fees or independent subscriptions with a business, or a business subscription to Nebula. If you need to cancel one of those services, specify which one so the appropriate channel can be identified. This distinction does not limit your personal data rights.

To request a review of retained information, understand applicable legal deadlines or file a complaint, see the rights and contact section of the privacy policy. Account deletion must not be interpreted as automatic erasure of every related record.
