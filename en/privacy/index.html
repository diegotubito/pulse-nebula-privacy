Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Pulse Nebula is a business operations platform for organizations, their authorized staff and customers to whom each business grants access. Depending on the workspace and enabled capabilities, it supports cash operations, sales, payments, expenses, inventory, memberships, customer orders, subscriptions, roles, and audit records.

Scope: Web, mobile apps and gym equipment

This policy distinguishes Nebula Web features, the Android and iOS mobile apps, and integrations with a business's physical equipment. Features and data processing depend on the business, permissions, platform and available release. A feature exclusive to Web or to a gym reader does not imply that the mobile app captures the same data.

Gym facial recognition takes place in its readers and is administered through Nebula Web, with a local connector where applicable. It is independent of the phone's biometric authentication. The two processes are described separately below.

Data Collected

This notice covers Nebula accounts and the features enabled for different types of businesses. For account management, its own security and support, the service provider identified below is responsible for those processing activities. When Nebula processes data on a business's instructions to administer its operations, that business determines the purpose of that processing and must inform its customers, staff and contacts. You may contact us to identify the party responsible for your request.

  • Account and identity data: username, first and last name, email address, user ID, authentication and security settings.
  • Device and notification data: an app-generated device identifier, push notification token, platform, operating-system and app version, locale, time zone, and device/session metadata.
  • Business and user-provided content: workspace and branch information; names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and tax identifiers entered for parties, customers, members, suppliers or contacts; operational notes and other content users submit.
  • Operational and financial records: cash movements, sales, payments, expenses, inventory activity, orders, subscriptions, membership collections, transaction amounts, references and statuses, and audit history. This data may be viewed or managed through the Web or mobile features enabled by the business. Pulse Nebula does not request or store full card numbers or security codes in the mobile app; card details are entered on the selected provider's payment page.
  • Security and request data: IP address, request metadata, authentication events and actions needed to prevent abuse, operate access controls and preserve auditability.
  • Supervised access and attendance, when enabled: the person's relationship with the business, branch, date and time, entry attempts and results. The branch associated with a record is not the device's GPS location; the record alone does not guarantee a person's physical presence or passage.

How We Use Data

  • Authenticate users and manage accounts, workspaces, roles and authorized devices.
  • Provide the business functions requested by users and keep operational records consistent and auditable.
  • Send account, security and operational notifications when enabled.
  • Protect the service, investigate failures and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Service Providers and Data Transfers

We use infrastructure, database, email and push-notification providers to operate Pulse Nebula. When a workspace enables a payment provider or a user starts a hosted checkout, the data needed to create and reconcile that transaction may be sent to that provider. These parties process data under their own terms or as service providers for the relevant operation. We do not sell personal data and Pulse Nebula does not contain advertising or use the Android Advertising ID.

Web payments and links from mobile

The mobile apps do not integrate payment collection SDKs or purchases through Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase, and do not sell Nebula software plans or add-ons. If the business enables payments for in-person services, such as a gym membership, the app may open an external page hosted by its payment provider. That provider processes the data you enter there under its privacy policy. Nebula processes the operational information needed to initiate, view and reconcile the payment, such as the person or membership reference, amount and status; the absence of a payment collection SDK does not mean Nebula does not process financial records.

Mobile apps: permissions and local phone biometrics

The mobile app may request camera access to scan workspace or branch access QR codes and notification permission for enabled push notifications. It does not use that camera to enroll or recognize faces in the gym's readers. The mobile experience described here does not request device location, microphone, contacts, advertising ID or broad storage access.

When biometric confirmation of supervised access is enabled, the app requests authentication from the phone's operating system, for example through Face ID or a fingerprint. Nebula does not access the images, fingerprints or biometric templates used by the operating system and does not receive them on its servers. The app uses local confirmation to continue the access flow; the operational result of that access may be recorded in Nebula. This authentication does not enroll the person in the gym's facial system.

Nebula Web and gym facial readers

This section applies only when the gym enables that integration. The gym determines the purpose of access control and administers its readers. It must inform people about the processing, establish the applicable legal basis and obtain consent where required, offer an alternative without facial recognition, and handle requests about data under its control. Nebula provides its administration and processing functions and remains responsible for its own obligations.

  • In the readers: facial capture and matching take place, and the enrollment data needed to recognize the person is retained, including biometric templates and images the equipment keeps according to its configuration. These are not captured through the Android or iOS apps.
  • In the gym's local connector, when synchronization is enabled: the connector temporarily processes one person's facial data in memory to transfer it between authorized readers at that installation. This flow does not save biometric content in connector files, queues or logs and does not send it to the Nebula cloud. Protection of the computer and local network also depends on the installation and its security controls.
  • In Nebula Web and its servers: administration covers person and reader identifiers, branch, authorizations, enrollment states, synchronization or deletion tasks, dates, access results and audit records. This flow does not send or store facial images or biometric templates in the Nebula cloud or display them in the browser.

Revoking access in Nebula does not itself prove that a reader has already erased its local data. Deletion must be confirmed on the relevant equipment; it may remain pending if a reader is offline or unresponsive. To request access, correction or deletion of this data, contact the gym or write to the email address below so that we can coordinate the request.

Security and Retention

Communications with Nebula servers use HTTPS. Connections between the connector and readers operate on the gym's local network and may use a protocol without HTTPS; they require a controlled network, isolation and the installation's security measures. Protection of the connection to Nebula should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every link to the gym's physical equipment is encrypted.

We retain account and operational data while it is needed to provide the service, protect accounts, preserve workspace records, and meet legal, security and audit obligations. Financial and operational history may be retained as immutable workspace records even after an individual user account is deleted. Data in readers has a separate retention and deletion lifecycle that the gym must manage; it is not automatically erased when a Nebula account is deleted.

Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion directly in the app from Settings > Delete account or initiate a request without the app through the account deletion page. Successful deletion removes the user account, its personal account record and refresh tokens. It does not delete business, financial or audit records owned by a workspace when those records must remain available to the organization or be retained for legal, security or audit purposes.

Contact

Your rights and how to exercise them

You may request access, correction, updating or deletion of your personal data, where applicable, by writing to pulsenebula.tech@gmail.com or the contact address below. Explain your request and provide the related email address or username. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary sensitive documents. Identity verification depends on the scope and risk of the request. If you no longer have access to your registered email, let us know so that another verification method can be assessed.

Requests about data processed for a business require coordination with that business as the responsible party; this does not remove Nebula's own obligations. Account deletion does not automatically erase all related data. You may request a review of remaining data and the reasons for retaining it.

In Argentina, access is free at intervals of at least six months, unless a legitimate interest is established, and the law provides ten calendar days from formal notice to respond. Correction, updating or deletion is subject to five business days under the conditions set out in law. Retention exceptions must be assessed against the particular information and applicable obligations; there is no blanket authorization to keep all data indefinitely.

The Argentine Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), the supervisory authority under Law 25,326, handles complaints from people whose personal data rights are affected by noncompliance with applicable data protection rules. See the AAIP guidance on rights and complaints (in Spanish).
